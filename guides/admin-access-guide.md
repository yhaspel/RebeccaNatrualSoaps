# Logging In

The site lives at **https://rebeccanaturalsoaps-production.up.railway.app**.

There are two ways to log in: as a regular customer, or as an admin.

## As a regular customer

1. Open the site.
2. Click **Sign in** in the top-right.
3. If you don't have an account yet, click **Create account** and fill in
   your email, name, and a password. Your account is ready right away —
   no email confirmation needed.
4. Once signed in, the **Account** page shows your past orders.

That's it — you can now browse, add to cart, and check out as a logged-in
customer.

## As an admin

Admins manage the shop itself: products, orders, and messages.

1. Go to **/admin/login** (i.e.
   https://rebeccanaturalsoaps-production.up.railway.app/admin/login).
2. Enter your admin username and password.
3. You'll land on the products page inside the admin area.

If you forget your admin password, ask whoever set up the site — they can
reset it for you.

## What an admin can do

The admin area has three sections, shown in the left-hand menu:

### Products

This is where you'll spend most of your time.

- **Add a product.** Click *New product*, fill in the name and description
  in both English and Hebrew, set the price, stock count, ingredients,
  pick a category, and upload an image. Save.
- **Edit a product.** Click any product in the list. Change anything you
  like — name, price, photo, ingredients, stock — and save. Changes are
  live immediately.
- **Hide a product.** Open the product and toggle *Active* off. It
  disappears from the storefront without being deleted, so you can bring
  it back later.
- **Delete a product.** Open the product and click *Delete*. This is
  permanent — prefer hiding unless you really want it gone.
- **Featured products.** Toggle *Featured* on a product to show it on the
  home page.

### Orders

Every order placed on the site appears here. You can see what was bought,
who bought it, where it should ship, and update the order's status as you
fulfill it (e.g. *paid* → *shipped*).

### Messages

Anything submitted through the contact form on the site shows up here. You
can read each message and mark it as resolved once you've replied by email.
